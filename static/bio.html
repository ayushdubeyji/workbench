The Electron Dance Inside Silicon

How do billions of microscopic switches compute without a single moving mechanical part? We demystify bandgap physics, quantum tunneling, and PN junctions into tangible hands-on intuition.

Why doesn't your processor melt? In modern CMOS logic, energy is primarily consumed only during state transitions (flipping between 0 and 1). Energy dissipation is dynamic capacitance, not static loss! Understanding thermodynamics transforms abstract gate diagrams into a living, breathing dance of electrons.
